This is the current version of the Machine Learning Foundations course first delivered in September 2025 at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology (DeKUT), Nyeri, Kenya.

The ML Foundations Course is designed to prepare those with a basic knowledge of probability and linear algebra for other Cambridge courses on machine learning. On completion of this course you should be able to follow the following courses:

  • Advanced Data Science
  • ML and the Physical World
  • Deep Neural Networks
